Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At Click Cookies, available from clickcookies.github.io, your privacy is highly valuable to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the simple types of information collected and how it is used.

1. Local Browser Storage

Click Cookies is an idle browser-based game. To ensure you do not lose your game progress, your game status (including baked cookie counts, purchased upgrades, bought buildings, and unlocked achievements) is preserved exclusively inside your browser's LocalStorage. This data stays native to your physical device and is never uploaded onto external servers by us.

2. Log Files & Automated Cookies

Like many web spaces, Click Cookies implements standard logging procedures. These logs capture visitor metrics when visiting the platform. Information within log files encompasses Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser variants, Internet Service Providers (ISP), timestamps, and referring/exit loops. These data sets remain unlinked to personally identifiable metrics, utilizing them strictly for user path routing and platform health checks.

3. Google Analytics and Tracking

We leverage Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand visitor behaviors and interaction loads across our application. Google uses analytical cookies to evaluate domain interactions. The insight generated regarding web interactions is stored on servers managed by Google. You possess complete capabilities to bypass tracking scripts by formatting specialized privacy blockades or disabling cookies within standard browser mechanics.

4. Third-Party Advertisements

Currently, Click Cookies remains ad-free and contains no external advertising modules. Should network infrastructure change down the line, ad servers may deploy integrated identifiers directly targeting standard browsers, dynamically tracking interaction behaviors to index contextual advertising targets. We maintain no control parameters over tracking components implemented via exterior marketing channels.

5. Children's Informational Protection

Providing digital safeguards for minors interacting online is an ethical priority. Click Cookies intentionally avoids recording identifiable information from individuals under thirteen years of age. If parents or guardians identify accidental collection occurrences within tracking records, please contact us swiftly to clean log history cleanly.

6. Universal Consent Agreements

By engaging with our structural design elements, you implicitly recognize the processing rules specified inside this documentation.
