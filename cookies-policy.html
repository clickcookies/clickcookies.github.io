Cookies Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

While Click Cookies revolves around virtual, delicious baking rewards[cite: 2], this policy explains how our web application uses digital data storage tokens to provide a functional gaming environment.

1. Virtual Cookies vs. Digital Cookies

To put it simply, our application focuses entirely on virtual cookies that you click to generate passive income[cite: 2]. However, to run correctly on the modern web, we also rely on technical digital storage keys (such as LocalStorage variables and cookies) to ensure you do not lose your score values when closing your tab[cite: 2].

2. Use of Browser LocalStorage

Instead of transmitting your user history to an external database, Click Cookies saves your gameplay metrics natively inside your web browser using client-side LocalStorage layers[cite: 2]. This technology remembers:

  • Your accumulated total cookie balance[cite: 2].
  • Your unlocked cookie types, buildings, and building counts[cite: 2].
  • All current purchased upgrades and earned achievements[cite: 2].

This data stays purely on your physical device and can be removed at any time by wiping your browser history or incognito session profiles[cite: 2].

3. Third-Party Analytics Trackers

We work alongside third-party service elements, notably Google Analytics, which set persistent digital trackers in your browser[cite: 2]. These identifiers gather anonymized insight about traffic routes, total unique users, and interactive load times to guide our technical SEO updates.

4. Managing and Clearing Your Data

You have full authority to modify your data setup. If you wish to deny tracking profiles or flush local device cache files, you can configure your browser to reject cookies or purge temporary data. Please note that clearing LocalStorage will permanently reset your gameplay progress back to zero[cite: 2].
